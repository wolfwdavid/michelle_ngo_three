- The hero poster is `static/posters/vimeo-264677021.jpg` — despite the `.jpg`

  @sveltejs/enhanced-img — enhanced-img is only used by PosterImage.svelte for the
  reel, never for the hero). `as="image"` preloads are format-agnostic, so the
  WebP-in-.jpg name does NOT break the preload.
- IMPLICATION: the LCP problem is NOT image weight. 16KB / 640x360 is already tiny.
  The miss is a RENDER-TIMING problem: measured FCP=1817ms but LCP=2806ms — a ~990ms
  gap where the poster downloads/paints LATE because its preload sits BEHIND
  SvelteKit's ~25 JS modulepreload <link>s. Do NOT spend effort shrinking the image
